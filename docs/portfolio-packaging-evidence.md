## Claims that remain intentionally unmade

- Packaging changes have not yet caused star growth; the measurement window begins after release.
- Stars do not prove engineering quality, correctness, or professional capability.
- Clean-checkout CI does not prove behavioural quality across every live model or environment.
- Synthetic examples demonstrate product mechanics, not production outcomes.
- GitHub traffic and visitor-to-star conversion are not available through this run.

## Next measurement

At 7 and 30 days, record:

- total stars and net-new stars per repository;
- GitHub unique visitors and referring sites when available;
- which repository receives the highest conversion;
- issues, forks, and external usage evidence;
- repeated first-run failures or questions.

Use those observations to decide whether the next constraint is discovery, activation, proof, or continued product value.
